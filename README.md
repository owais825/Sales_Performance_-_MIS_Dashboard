# Sales Performance & MIS Dashboard | Advanced Excel

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing retail sales data and building an interactive MIS dashboard using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profit, quantity, and business performance across different product categories, customer segments, regions, and time periods.

The objective is to make sales performance easier to monitor through KPI reporting, trend analysis, and year-over-year comparisons.

## Dataset

- **Dataset:** Superstore_Dataset
- **Reporting Period:** 2014–2017
- **Data Type:** Retail sales and order transactions

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Formulas
- Interactive Charts
- Dashboard Design
- KPI Reporting
- Data Analysis and Visualization

## Dashboard Features

### 1. KPI Monitoring
- Sales performance
- Total profit
- Quantity sold
- Overall business performance

### 2. Sales Trend Analysis
- Year-wise sales and profit trends
- Quarterly sales performance
- Sales growth analysis
- Year-over-year comparisons

### 3. Product Performance Analysis
- Category-wise sales and profit
- Sub-category performance
- Identification of product-level performance differences

### 4. Regional & Customer Analysis
- City and state-level sales performance
- Regional performance comparison
- Customer segment analysis
- Shipping mode analysis

### 5. Interactive Dashboard
- Category filters
- Customer segment filters
- Shipping mode filters
- Interactive charts and reporting views

## Key Business Findings

- Recorded approximately **20.4% sales growth in 2017** compared with 2016.
- Observed approximately **14.2% profit growth in 2017** compared with 2016.
- Analysed sales and profit performance across **4 geographical regions**.
- Evaluated business performance across a four-year reporting period.

## Business Applications

This dashboard demonstrates how Excel can be used for:

- Preparing regular MIS reports.
- Monitoring sales and profit KPIs.
- Tracking business performance across regions.
- Identifying sales trends and product-level performance.
- Comparing current and previous year performance.
- Supporting data-driven business reporting.
